Our Story

New agency.
Experienced hands.

CRM Flow Lab started with a simple observation: most practices don’t need more marketing — they need to stop dropping the leads they already get.

Before the agency, we built 50+ GoHighLevel systems for paying clients across industries — funnels, pipelines, automations, the unglamorous plumbing that makes businesses run. Along the way we noticed something: appointment-based practices got the biggest, fastest wins from the same handful of systems.

So we niched down. Dental clinics and med spas. Two kinds of practices, one playbook refined until it’s boring — in the best way. Boring means it works every time.
