No-shows aren’t a patient problem — they’re a reminder problem. We set up layered reminders with one-tap confirm, cancel, and reschedule, plus automatic waitlist backfill when someone cancels.

  • ✓ One-tap confirm / reschedule links
  • ✓ Waitlist backfill for cancelled slots
  • ✓ No-show follow-up to rebook automatically
