Med Spa Rebooking Guide

Med spa rebooking texts
6 reminders that fill calendars

Why clients say they will rebook and then vanish, six texts you can adapt, when to send them and how to automate it.

By Usman Ali, founder of CRM Flow Lab · Published November 11, 2026

Quick answer: After each treatment, send an aftercare message, then a short “time for your next session” text with a booking link when the client is due. Keep it friendly, keep health details out of the message, send one gentle follow-up and stop once the client books or opts out. A CRM can send all of it automatically.

A client loves her facial, says she will rebook, and then nothing happens. Without a rebooking sequence, “see you soon” can quietly turn into “see you never.” We don’t quote conversion figures here, because results depend on your spa and your treatments. The idea is simple: make the next booking one tap away at the right time.

Why do med spa clients stop rebooking?

  • Nobody reminds them. Clients are busy, and the next session slips their mind.
  • Booking takes effort. If rebooking means a phone call during work hours, many clients never do it.
  • The follow-up depends on a person. When the front desk is busy, rebooking messages are the first thing to be forgotten.

What rebooking texts can you copy?

Replace the bracketed parts with your own details. Keep treatment and health details out of the text itself.

1. Aftercare check-in (day after treatment).
“Hi [First name], thanks for visiting [Spa name]! If you have any questions after your visit, just reply here and our team will help.”

2. Time for your next session.
“Hi [First name], it’s about time for your next session at [Spa name]. Pick a time that suits you: [Booking link]”

3. Gentle follow-up.
“Hi [First name], just a quick reminder that your next session is due. You can book in under a minute here: [Booking link]”

4. Membership member reminder.
“Hi [First name], your member session is ready to book. Choose your time here: [Booking link]”

5. Offer a cancelled slot.
“Hi [First name], a slot just opened up on [Day, time]. Want it? Reply YES and we’ll hold it for you.”

6. Last note.
“Hi [First name], we don’t want to bother you, so this is our last reminder. Whenever you’re ready, our link is always here: [Booking link]”

When should you send the reminders?

  • After treatment: an aftercare message first, so the relationship feels caring rather than salesy.
  • When the client is due: in our med spa example, a rebooking nudge goes out around week 6. Adjust it to each treatment.
  • One follow-up, then stop: do not keep messaging someone who is not responding.
  • Stop rules: end the sequence when the client books, replies or opts out.

What should you check before automating?

  • Consent. Make sure clients agree to receive texts, and honor opt-outs straight away.
  • Privacy. Do not mention treatments or health details in the text. Check your obligations with your own advisor.
  • A human handoff. When a client replies with a question, a person should answer.
  • One clear action. Each text asks for one thing: booking the next session.

How do you automate rebooking?

  • Trigger the sequence from the treatment and date in your CRM.
  • Send the aftercare message automatically, then the rebooking nudge when the client is due.
  • Link to a booking page with real-time availability.
  • Offer cancelled slots to a waitlist.
  • Stop automatically when the client books, replies or opts out.
